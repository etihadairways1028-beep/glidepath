Privacy

Last updated 26 November 2026

In short: without an account, nothing about you leaves your own PC and devices. With a free account, we keep your email, your logbook and your settings so they follow you, and nothing else. We don't sell data, show adverts or use tracking cookies. Delete your account in the app and it's all gone.

Without an account

The Bridge on your PC reads your flight from Microsoft Flight Simulator and sends it to your own devices on your own network. Your flights, logbook and settings are kept on your PC and in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.

With a Glidepath account

If you create an account, we store:

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose, and your password, kept only as a salted hash (we can't read it).
  • Sign-ins: a record of each signed-in device (a random key, kept as a hash, and when it was last used), so you stay signed in. A count of recent failed sign-ins for your email, to slow down password guessing, cleared after 15 minutes.
  • Your logbook: a summary of each flight (route, times, aircraft, distance, fuel, landing and review results), so it shows on all your devices.
  • Connect anywhere: if you sign in on the Bridge, a key for that PC (kept as a hash) and the PC's name. While your devices are connected, your live flight passes through your account's relay to reach them; it isn't stored.
  • Email settings: which emails you want, and a note of which ones were sent, so you don't get the same one twice.
  • Glidepath AI: a count of how many questions you asked each day, for the free daily limit, kept for three days. Your questions and answers aren't stored.
  • Synced settings: your app settings (like units and theme) and the procedures you picked, so a change on one device shows on the others.
  • Lock screen notifications: only if you turn them on, for each device: the address your browser gives us to deliver notifications to it, its keys, and the device type. A note of which notifications each flight has had, so you don't get the same one twice.
  • Live links and the landing challenge: only if you turn them on. A live link shows your position to people with the link until the flight ends; the challenge shows your best landing of the month under the name you choose.

Emails

We send the emails you've chosen: your debrief, top of descent and arrival, new sign-ins, and password resets. They're sent through an email delivery service (Google, Mailjet or Brevo, depending on how Glidepath is set up), which receives your address and the message to deliver it. Turn each kind off in the app under Account.

Notifications

Notifications are delivered by your browser's own push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device). The message is encrypted so that only your device can read it; the push service sees only where to deliver it. Turn notifications off in Settings, or in your device's settings, at any time.

Glidepath AI

When you ask Copilot a question while signed in, your question, the conversation so far and your current flight's numbers (position, altitude, speeds, phase, plan, weather) are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to write the answer. Your email and account details aren't sent. Glidepath doesn't store the conversation; Cloudflare handles the request under its own privacy policy. If you've connected your own AI model to the Bridge, questions go to that model instead.

Where it's kept

Accounts and the relay run on Cloudflare. The website and the app are served by Cloudflare and GitHub; the Bridge download comes from GitHub. Every few hours the Bridge also fetches the newest copy of the app from the Glidepath website, so devices that open it from your PC get the current version; that request carries nothing about you or your flights. These services see the usual connection details (like your IP address) to deliver pages, as any website does.

Things your device fetches directly

To show maps, weather and traffic, the app asks these services itself: map tiles (OpenStreetMap, Esri World Imagery), weather (aviationweather.gov, RainViewer), online traffic and ATC (VATSIM, IVAO, SayIntentions), airport data (OurAirports), and SimBrief, if you add your SimBrief name. The live camera uses free public STUN servers (Cloudflare and Google) to help your devices find each other; the picture itself goes straight between your devices, encrypted.

Cookies

No tracking or advertising cookies. The app keeps your settings and sign-in in your browser's storage, only so it works.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Account and choose Delete account. Your account, logbook, sign-ins, PC keys, live links, challenge entries, synced settings, notification devices and AI counts are deleted straight away. Data kept only on your PC and devices stays there until you remove it.
